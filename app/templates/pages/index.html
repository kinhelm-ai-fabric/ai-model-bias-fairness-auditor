AI Model Bias & Fairness Auditor

Audit Your AI Models for Bias

Upload model predictions alongside demographic data to compute fairness metrics such as disparate impact, statistical parity, and equal opportunity. Generate AI-powered reports with actionable recommendations.

Start New Audit Getting Started

How It Works

  1. Upload a CSV with predictions, labels, and a protected attribute column.
  2. Select the protected attribute (e.g., gender, race, age group).
  3. Run the analysis to compute fairness metrics automatically.
  4. Generate an AI-powered audit report with findings and recommendations.

Metrics Computed

  • Disparate Impact Ratio — Ratio of selection rates between groups (threshold: 0.8)
  • Statistical Parity Difference — Difference in positive prediction rates
  • Equal Opportunity Difference — Difference in true positive rates
  • Per-group Selection Rate — Fraction predicted favorable per group
  • Per-group TPR & FPR — True/false positive rates per group
